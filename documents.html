Documents

What we issue with every shipment, and what we need from you. Ask for any of them in the request form and we send copies by email.

Waybill (consignment note)

Issued at pick-up. Lists sender, receiver, weight, pieces and special handling.

Proof of delivery

Signed by the receiver on the day of delivery. Sent as a scan within two hours.

Commercial invoice

Needed for customs. Send us the invoice with item descriptions, values and origin.

Packing list

Needed for international shipments. One line per package with weight and dimensions.

Customs declaration

Prepared by our broker. You confirm the figures before it is submitted.

Temperature log

For refrigerated cargo. Readings every 15 minutes from pick-up to delivery.

Documents we ask for

For a smooth customs clearance, have these ready before the shipment is collected.

Shipment typeYou send us
Domestic parcelReceiver name, address and phone

Import of goodsInvoice, contract copy and product codes
