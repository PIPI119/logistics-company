Services

Each service has its own scope, delivery window and a contact person who follows it to the end.

Road freight

Full and part loads, pallets and oversize cargo. Regional and international routes, with tracking by shipment number.

Express parcels

Parcels up to 30 kg. Same-day pick-up inside the city, next-day delivery inside the region.

Warehouse storage

Short-term storage at our hubs with a daily stock report and pick-and-pack for online orders.

Customs support

Export and import declarations, duty estimates and a broker who meets the cargo at the border.

Temperature cargo

Refrigerated vans for food and pharmacy. Temperature logged every 15 minutes and sent with the delivery note.

Cargo insurance

Optional insurance per shipment, priced as a percentage of the declared value and added to the quote.

Not sure which service fits?

Describe the cargo in the request form. We suggest the service and send a fixed quote.
